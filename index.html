Privacy Policy

How Collection Import Export, developed by Lake Members, handles the limited store data it needs to export, import and sync your Shopify collections.

No customer data GDPR & CCPA Shopify privacy webhooks Data never sold

Quick summary: what data does Collection Import Export access?

  • ✓Store and collection data only. Your shop domain, an access token, and collection details (titles, handles, rules, sort orders, image URLs, and associated product IDs or handles) needed to run exports, imports and syncs.
  • ✓Job history. Timestamps, statuses, item counts and error logs, so you can review past operations.
  • ✕Customer data is never accessed. No names, emails, phone numbers, addresses or purchase history.
  • ✕No orders, checkout or payment data. Billing goes through the Shopify Billing API, so we never see card details.
  • ✓Deleted within 48 hours of uninstalling the app or receiving a shop/redact request. Your data is never sold or rented.

#Introduction and overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Lake Members ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you install and use Collection Import Export (the "App") on your Shopify store.

The App lets Shopify merchants export, import and sync product collections, both automated (smart) collections and manual collections, between stores.

Scope

This policy applies to merchants and their authorized staff who install or use the App, and to our support channels. It does not cover Shopify or other third-party services, which have their own policies.

Agreement

By installing or using the App, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not install or use the App.

Access scopes

The App requests only three scopes: read_products, write_products and read_product_listings.

Cookies and local storage

The App runs embedded inside the Shopify admin. We use only the essential session cookies and browser local or session storage needed to keep you signed in and remember basic interface state. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Support communications

If you email us at starcjosh5@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address and store URL, so we can respond to your request.

#Information we do not collect

No customer personal data

The App does not collect, process or store any customer personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, or shipping and billing addresses.
  • No customer purchase history or browsing behavior.
  • No access to checkout, orders or payment credentials.
  • No credit card or other payment details. All billing is processed by Shopify through the Shopify Billing API.

Because the App holds no customer data, there is nothing to disclose or erase when Shopify's customer privacy webhooks arrive. We still respond to them, as described in the retention section below.

#How we use information

  • Running export and import jobs: reading collection data from your store and writing imported collections back to it.
  • Enabling cross-store sync: using temporary OTP codes and linked store domains to connect stores and copy collections between them.
  • Showing job history: displaying statuses, counts and errors so you can review past operations.
  • Keeping the App reliable: diagnosing errors, preventing abuse and improving performance.
  • Managing plans: applying the features that match your active subscription.
  • Providing support: responding to your questions and requests.
  • Meeting legal obligations: complying with Shopify's requirements and applicable law.

We do not use merchant data for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to operating the App.

#Third-party services and hosting

We rely on a limited set of service providers to run the App:

  • Shopify: the App integrates with the Shopify Admin API for authentication, data access and billing.
  • Hosting infrastructure: secure cloud servers that run the App.
  • Cloud database: managed database storage for session, job and connection records.
We never sell or rent your data

Providers act only on our instructions and process data solely to deliver their services to us. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the security of the App.

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Automatic deletion on uninstall

When you uninstall the App, or when we receive a shop/redact request from Shopify, all access tokens, connection records and store-specific job logs are permanently deleted within 48 hours.

Shopify mandatory privacy webhooks

The App complies with Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks:

  • customers/data_request: we hold no customer data, so there is nothing to return.
  • customers/redact: we hold no customer data, so there is nothing to erase.
  • shop/redact: all store-specific data is permanently deleted as described above.

#Merchant rights

Depending on where you are located, including under the GDPR (EEA and UK) and the CCPA/CPRA (California), you may have these rights over your information:

  • Access: ask for confirmation of the data we hold about your store and a copy of it.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your data. Uninstalling the App triggers automatic deletion.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Restriction and objection: ask us to limit or stop certain processing.
  • No sale of personal information: we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not discriminate against anyone for exercising their rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email starcjosh5@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

#Security safeguards

  • Encryption in transit: all communication between your browser, Shopify and our servers uses SSL/TLS.
  • Secure token storage: access tokens are stored securely and used only for authorized API calls for your store.
  • Restricted access: only authorized personnel with a legitimate need can reach systems that store merchant data.
  • OTP expiration: store-to-store connection codes are single-purpose and expire automatically after 10 minutes.
  • Minimal scopes: the App requests only read_products, write_products and read_product_listings.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by law.

#Children's privacy

The App is a business tool for Shopify merchants and is not directed to children or minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 (or the applicable age of majority). If you believe a minor has given us information, contact starcjosh5@gmail.com and we will delete it.
